# 28 — Owner Guide — Vantrilex Next-Gen Agentic Workbench & Launcher

## Purpose

The non-technical owner's manual for daily use.

## Status

Written in plain words. Technical detail lives in the numbered docs.

## Schema

### Daily use

Launch the app, pick Guided Smart Project, choose a folder, confirm the
detected case, press Enter. The app provisions docs, tools, and skills,
then starts your agent. Speak or type; approve steps on your phone.

### Keys and phone

Add Fish Audio and Groq keys once via the credential prompt for voice.
Pair your phone once with the QR code. The app handles rotation and
reconnection by itself.

### When something breaks

Read the on-screen message first. Re-run provisioning to repair docs and
tools. Check 14-RUNBOOK.md procedures for persistent issues. Nothing you
do in guided mode can delete your files: provisioning never overwrites.
